Statement of Elena Ruiz (S-14)
Taken 2025-06-02 by Inv. M. Tran, DA Investigations.

On the evening of April 18 I was waiting for the bus at Alder and 9th. Around 10:15 pm I heard two men arguing outside the Quick Stop. The taller one, in a tan work jacket, shoved the other into the newspaper box. I looked away for a second and when I looked back the shorter man was on the ground. The man in the tan jacket walked off toward the rail underpass.

I have read this statement and it is true.
Signature: Elena Ruiz
